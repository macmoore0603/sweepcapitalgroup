# US evidence, 2026-11-03

All 20 key figures were re-read on their sources by an independent check, and all 20 were confirmed.

## Retail traders in US futures

- **CFTC Office of the Chief Economist staff study** (Ferko, Mixon & Onur, February 2024; 36,538 retail accounts at one large broker, February 2021 to November 2022):
  - Micro Nasdaq-100 was the second most-held contract.
  - The median trading episode lost $95 to $155, and traders broke even only around the 60th percentile.
  - A big first loss predicted quitting, so the traders who remain are survivors.
- **E-mini S&P** (Baron, Brogaard & Kirilenko): the persistent winners were high-frequency firms. New entrants underperformed and left.

## Day traders and active traders

- **Jordan & Diltz** (*Financial Analysts Journal* 2003; 324 day traders, 1998-99): 35.8% were profitable after commissions. The average gross profit of $8,000 became about -$750 net.
- **Garvey & Murphy** (2005; 1,386 accounts, March-June 2000): about half were profitable, and winners and losers traded alike. A 15-person prop team made money by providing liquidity inside the spread, holding about 3 minutes, not by predicting direction.
- **Harris & Schultz** (*JFE* 1998, SOES bandits): commissions took about 70% of gross profit, and holds longer than 5 minutes lost money.
- **Barber & Odean** (*JF* 2000; 66,465 households): the most active fifth earned 11.4% a year net against 17.9% for the market. Gross returns were the same; costs made the gap.
- **Retail options:** -$2.1 billion, November 2019 to June 2021 (Bryzgalova, Pavlova & Sikorskaya, *JF* 2023).

## US regulators

- **NASAA 1999** (state regulators' day-trading review): 70% of the accounts reviewed lost money, and trading costs averaged 56% of equity a year.
- **Retail forex dealers' required quarterly disclosures:** about two thirds of accounts lose every quarter. OANDA reported 65.7-68.3% and FOREX.com 65.8-69.1% in 2025-2026. Futures have no such required disclosure.
- **Prop firms:** no US regulator has published an alert, rule or outcome data on futures prop-firm or "funded trader" programs.
- **CFTC v. Traders Global Group (My Forex Funds)**, the only enforcement test:
  - The complaint alleged $310 million or more in fees and about $137 million paid out, and that fewer than 100 of 24,000+ "live" accounts ever had a trade routed to a market.
  - The case was dismissed with prejudice in May 2025 as a sanction against the CFTC. The legality of the business model was never decided.
- **Pattern-day-trader rule:** FINRA's replacement took effect June 4, 2026, removing the $25,000 minimum for day trading stocks.

## For an NQ prop-firm day trader

- **Costs times trade count** is the main driver of results.
- **Documented intraday winners** provided liquidity; they did not predict direction.
- **The base rates are about 1 in 3 to 1 in 2:** that is the share profitable in any one period, and fewer stay profitable.
- **A prop payout is a contract claim** on a usually simulated account, not protected customer money.
